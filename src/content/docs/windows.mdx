<h3 style="color: #FF7900; font-weight: bold;">Important:</h3>

<span style="font-weight: bold;">
    In this early Alpha version of InterShare for Windows, <u>file transfers</u> are supported only when both devices are on the <u>same Wi-Fi network.</u>

    However, your Windows PC still <u>requires Bluetooth to be enabled</u> because the discovery of nearby devices currently relies on Bluetooth.
    It's important to note that <u>Bluetooth is only used for device discovery on Windows</u>; the actual <u>file transfers</u> over Bluetooth are not yet supported. For now, all file transfers occur via Wi-Fi.

    If you're curious about the technical limitations around Bluetooth transfers on Windows, check out our detailed explanation [here](/blog/windows-alpha#bluetooth-on-windows-a-tragedy).
</span>

# Sharing Files

![Send files](/blog/windows-alpha/windows-start.png)

Simply drag and drop any files you want to share into the designated area, or click the corresponding button to select them manually.

After selecting the files, nearby devices will appear. Discovery may take a few seconds to complete.

![blog placeholder](/blog/windows-alpha/windows-send.png)

Ensure that the receiver is on <u>the same Wi-Fi network</u>. Once the receiver appears, simply click on their device to start sharing.

## Receiving Files on Windows

To receive files on Windows, open the InterShare app and remain on the start page. When someone sends you a file, you will receive a notification in the bottom-right corner of your screen.

Once the download is complete, you will find the received files in your Downloads folder.
